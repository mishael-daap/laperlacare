Perla Care brand book

Warm, calm and trustworthy. The identity is built from a gold pearl held by a smile, set in soft greens. Use the files and tokens on this page exactly as written.

Logo

The mark is a gold pearl (a circle with a small cream highlight at its upper left) resting in a smile arc (a thick, round-capped U-shaped stroke). The wordmark is Perla Care in two colors: "Perla" in Forest, "Care" in Sage text. Text inside the SVGs is already converted to outlines, so it needs no font to render.

Usage rules

  • Clear space: keep at least the radius of the pearl (about 24% of the mark's width) free on all sides.
  • Minimum size: stacked logo 120px wide, horizontal logo 120px wide, icon 24px wide.
  • App icons and avatars: place the icon on a rounded square (radius about 22% of the side). Very light sage tile uses icon-forest.svg; Forest tile uses icon-light.svg.
  • Do not recolor the pearl, change the arc thickness, rotate the mark, stretch it, add shadows or gradients, or retype the wordmark in another font.
  • Do not place the logo on a busy photo or on Gold or Soft sage backgrounds.

Color

Soft sage#9DB89Argb(157, 184, 154)hsl(114, 17%, 66%)Role: secondary accents, horizontal logo arc, dividers.
Sage text#4F6E55rgb(79, 110, 85)hsl(132, 16%, 37%)Role: "Care" in the wordmark, secondary text, links.
Forest#2E4636rgb(46, 70, 54)hsl(140, 21%, 23%)Role: primary text, "Perla", dark surfaces, primary buttons.
Gold#E0A93Brgb(224, 169, 59)hsl(40, 73%, 55%)Role: the pearl. Use sparingly for one highlight per screen (badges, key icons).
Pearl highlight#F5E6C6rgb(245, 230, 198)hsl(42, 67%, 87%)Role: only the small shine inside the pearl. Sampled from the artwork.

Pairings that pass contrast: Forest on Very light sage, Forest on white, Very light sage on Forest, Sage text on white. Avoid Gold or Soft sage for body text.

Scale: 48 / 28 / 18 / 16 / 14px (h1, h2, h3, body, caption). Keep lines under 70 characters.

Style

  • Feel: soft, rounded, reassuring. Everything leans on curves: rounded corners, round-capped strokes, circles.
  • Corners: cards 24px radius, tiles 18px, small chips 10px. Buttons fully rounded or 12px.
  • Surfaces: flat color, no gradients, no heavy shadows. Use a 1px Very light sage border on white cards.
  • Color balance: roughly 60% light neutrals (white, Very light sage), 30% Forest and Sage text, 10% Soft sage, with Gold as a single accent.
  • Voice: warm, plain and caring. Short sentences, sentence case, no jargon.
