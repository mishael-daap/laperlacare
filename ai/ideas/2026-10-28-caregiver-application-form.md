## Join the Perla Care caregiver network

Perla Care connects Bay Area families of children and adults with special needs with trusted, verified caregivers for respite care. You set your own rate and schedule, and families contact you directly.

Who should apply: you are 18 or older and have at least 6 months of experience supporting people with special needs (paid or volunteer).

How to apply: fill out every section, sign section 6, and return this form by email or in person. We review every application within 5 business days.
